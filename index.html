About WorkTrack

Your personal engineering work management workspace.

  • Local browser storage remains the working cache.
  • Optional Google Drive sync through your own Apps Script deployment.
  • Dated JSON recovery snapshots are stored in your private Drive folder.
  • Calendar export creates an .ics file you can import.
  • Browser reminders still require browser permission and may not run reliably when the app is closed.
